Questions and answers

What is DSLKit?

DSLKit is a web editor for diagrams as code. You describe a scheme in DSL text, and the editor checks it against the notation rules and draws the diagram.

Do I need to know how to program?

No. The syntax is short, and notation templates and hints help you write your first diagram. A click-based editing mode is planned for those who do not write code.

Which notations will be supported?

Six notations are planned: IDEF0, DFD, ERD, BPMN, UML and Petri nets. IDEF0, DFD and ERD arrive first in the beta.

How much does it cost?

The beta is free, with up to five diagrams per day. The paid Pro plan with extended features will appear later.
