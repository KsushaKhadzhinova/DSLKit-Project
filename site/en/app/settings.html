Account and security

The account is kept in this browser only: there is no server. The password is protected by a PBKDF2 hash, but whoever opens the browser profile can read and change all data. This is a study model of accounts, not server-grade protection; see the documentation.

Sign in to manage your password, sessions and two-factor authentication. Sign in

Change password

After the change every other session is revoked.

Sessions

A session lives up to 7 days with "Remember me" and up to 12 hours without it; it ends after 30 minutes without activity.

    Two-factor authentication (TOTP)

    One-time codes of RFC 6238: SHA-1, 30 seconds, 6 digits. Any authenticator app works.
