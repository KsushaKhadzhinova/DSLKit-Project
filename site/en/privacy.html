Privacy policy

Updated October 13, 2026

DSLKit is a student project in beta development. This page explains what data we receive through the website, why we need it and how we handle it.

What data we collect

Through the beta sign-up form we ask for: name, email address, chosen notation, team size and a comment. The comment is optional; the other fields are needed to complete the form.

Why we use the data

The data is needed only to invite you to the beta version and to send messages about the launch of access. We do not use it for other purposes without separate consent.

Retention period

The data is kept until the beta version opens or until you ask us to delete it, but no longer than needed to launch the project. We will specify the exact period before access opens and inform you about it by email.

Sharing with third parties

We do not share your personal data with third parties and do not sell it.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, its correction or deletion, and withdraw your consent to emails about the beta launch. To do so, write to hello@dslkit.io. We will reply within a reasonable time.

Contact

For questions about data processing, write to hello@dslkit.io.
